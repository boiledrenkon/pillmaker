IMAGE PROMPT:

A fictional single-capsule vitality supplement package called "NIBBANA 6900".

The entire package is designed in the style of a sparse Zen ink painting (sumi-e), inspired by traditional Bodhidharma scroll paintings. Large areas of aged rice paper remain empty. The composition uses very few brush strokes. Every stroke is bold, thick, confident, and executed with a single fluid motion. Minimal shading. No realism. No detailed rendering. No digital painting aesthetics.

The artwork occupies only portions of the page, surrounded by vast negative space.

On the left side is a Bodhidharma-like Zen master rendered with only a handful of expressive black ink strokes. Bald head, fierce eyes, thick brows, beard, robe suggested rather than painted. His form emerges from the paper through abstraction and gesture rather than detail. The brushwork should resemble museum-quality Zen scroll paintings.

On the right side is a sensual female figure rendered in the same sparse brush style. Her eyes are closed and her expression is one of profound bliss, serenity, surrender, and awakening. She appears transformed or illuminated rather than merely aroused. Her body is suggested through elegant calligraphic curves and minimal linework. The figure should feel poetic, symbolic, and dreamlike.

Between them is a single metallic gold capsule sealed under realistic blister plastic. The capsule acts as the symbolic bridge between the two figures.

The package title "NIBBANA 6900" is painted directly onto the paper using the same brush and ink style as the artwork. The typography should feel hand-painted, imperfect, and expressive rather than designed. It should appear as if written by a monk using a large calligraphy brush.

Additional small text is minimal and integrated into the composition:

ENLIGHTENMENT SUPPORT
COSMIC VITALITY FORMULA
AWAKEN HER INNER BLISS

These lines should be subtle, secondary, and visually subordinate to the artwork.

Include vertical East Asian calligraphy columns near the top and sides similar to historical Bodhidharma scrolls. The calligraphy should appear naturally integrated into the composition rather than functioning as marketing copy.
