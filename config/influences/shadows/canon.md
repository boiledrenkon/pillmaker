# Influence canon — "shadows" lens

Works that shape this lens's aesthetic sensibility without being *about* the
product line. Each entry: the work, then a note on **what of it resonates** —
the note matters more than the title, because it steers WHICH thread of the
work gets distilled. These are influences on taste, never subject matter:
nothing here should ever appear on a pack as a setting, era, or reference.

Distill with `bun tools/distill_style.ts shadows` → writes
`config/lenses/shadows.md` (the sensibility document injected into compose when
a run selects this lens). Re-run whenever this file or `texts/` changes. To
create another lens, copy this folder shape to `config/influences/<name>/` —
a MIX of lenses is just a new lens whose canon lists the union.

---

- **In Praise of Shadows — Jun'ichirō Tanizaki (1933)**
  What resonates: beauty found in shadow, patina, and half-light rather than
  glare and polish; ornament that reveals itself slowly instead of announcing
  itself; the glow of a rich material seen dimly; suggestion over statement.
