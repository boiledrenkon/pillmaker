**Palette & light**
Let low, gathered light do the work. Prize deep tonal ranges where forms emerge from dimness and a few surfaces catch a soft, restrained glow. Favor muted, shadow-weighted hues over bright saturation, so that color reads as warmth held in reserve rather than announced.

**Material & finish**
Reach for the quiet richness of aged and lived-in surfaces — patina, depth, a finish that looks touched by time. Prize materials that glow faintly from within when seen dimly, rewarding a second look over an immediate shine.

**Typography & lettering**
Let lettering settle into the surface rather than sit on top of it. Prize marks that reveal themselves slowly, carrying quiet authority through restraint, spacing, and understatement instead of size or contrast.

**Composition**
Build around suggestion. Give shadow and empty space as much weight as the subject, letting the eye complete what is only partly shown. Prize ornament that discloses itself gradually, becoming richer the longer it is held.

**Temperament**
Overall, favor intimacy, discretion, and depth over glare and spectacle. Let the work speak softly and reveal itself in stages, trusting the viewer's patience. Prize beauty that lives in half-light and shows its full character only to those who linger.
